WPGT Mirante

A WikiProject GeoTwin tool by Rafael Lemieszek

Browse the Wikimedia Commons photos of a place in 3D. Structure-from-motion recovers where each photo was taken and which way it faced; moving between photos flies the camera along that path and cross-fades them over the shared 3D points. ←→↑↓ move, O opens the overview, F changes how cameras are drawn there.

Photos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons and stay under their authors' licences, shown with each photo. The 3D points and camera positions are derived from them and are released under CC BY-SA 4.0; full credits.
